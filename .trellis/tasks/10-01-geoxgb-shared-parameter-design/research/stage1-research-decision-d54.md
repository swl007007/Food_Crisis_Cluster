# Stage 1 research decision after D54 (2026-10-02)

Supervisor-authored decisions under the user's delegated Stage 1 research authority, recorded by the executor. **This memo does not authorise another run.**

## Decisions

1. **Engineering vs generalisation.** D52 model replays and D54 input alignment/numerical reconstruction passed their stated checks; this establishes those engineering checks, not statistical generalisation or whole-task acceptance.
2. **Root and partitions.** The root already trails persistence on matched E3 crisis F1, and the existing partition increments are not a robust rescue. D41 (frozen-map local halving) and D54 (the same local increments at the fixed D38 operating point) are limited controls, not a refutation of all partitions. (`research/d41-local-shrinkage-findings.md`, `research/d54-fixed-policy-local-contrast-findings.md`)
3. **Binary objective (D52).** The binary root raised FIT/C F1 but worsened E3 F1 at every horizon, and was not adopted. Capacity and Hessian differences confound any objective attribution. (`research/d52-binary-root-findings.md`)
4. **State-probability transfer (D53).** Level mismatch between FIT and E3 depends on state and horizon. Composition changes mean this is not causal proof of pure drift or pure overfitting, and no uniform offset fix follows. (`research/d53-state-probability-transfer-findings.md`)
5. **Persistence preference (D38).** The D38 post-hoc F1 gain trades a worse Brier against the raw root (matched .115976 vs .101885), so it is not a calibrated-probability claim. Anchored-root training was already tested in D38 and must not be presented as an untested simple solution. D54 did **not** train partitions on an anchored base. (`d38-persistence-margin-root-plan.md`)
6. **Explicit research stop on adjacent current-recipe variants.** No automatic D55; no threshold, alpha, class-weight or capacity grid; no full 648, Stage 2 formula, Stage 3, final evaluation or close.

## Status

- Stage 1 remains open. No claim is made that the expert or persistence final target has been met.
- A restart needs a distinct falsifiable mechanism and a written, bounded contrast whose transfer criterion is set before any scoring.
- The current exposed development outcomes (D26–D54) are diagnostic, not fresh confirmation.
