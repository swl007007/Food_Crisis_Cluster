## Official documentation checked

- [Regional reductions](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/reducers_reduce_region): an explicit scale or CRS/transform is required to avoid unintended grids. Weighted reducers use approximate polygon/pixel intersection fractions; the documentation specifies approximately `1/256 (~0.4%)` minimum overlap and 8-bit fractional-coverage quantization. These are boundary-weight approximations, not a guarantee on the overall error of an admin statistic. `bestEffort=true` can automatically increase scale to satisfy pixel limits.
- [Export.table.toCloudStorage](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/apidocs/export-table-tocloudstorage): a FeatureCollection can be exported as a batch table, with bucket/prefix, field selectors and formats including CSV. This establishes that raster export is not required to deliver admin statistics. It does not establish a usable destination, free compute or current project quota.
- [AlphaEarth catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_SATELLITE_EMBEDDING_V1_ANNUAL): native 10 m, 64 dimensions, local UTM images and source-version properties; see `alphaearth-documentation.md` for the already accepted 2017/moment/coverage contract.

The coordinator fetched the first two official pages read-only in this turn and inspected their aggregation/export definitions. No product data were fetched.

## Accepted option: native-grid EE aggregation and local statistical artifacts

1. After execution approval, inventory the intersecting **2017** assets for the existing 6,227 frozen polygons. Pin asset IDs, time coverage, dataset/model/processing versions, CRS and affine transforms before extraction. Do not silently substitute another year or mix incompatible product versions. The catalog's stated versions are expectations, not a completed live asset inventory.
2. Perform area-weighted sufficient-statistic aggregation on each source's native 10 m grid in EE. Preserve explicit projection/transform, the common 64-band finite-valid mask and D16's no-double-counting requirement across overlapping source footprints. Freeze deterministic overlap ownership independent of outcomes; do not independently sum overlapping full tiles. No coarsening through `bestEffort`, lower-resolution pyramid aggregation or mean-vector renormalization.
3. Accept and disclose EE's documented approximate boundary-intersection weights. D16/D25 are operationalized with these weights, not claimed to use mathematically exact polygon/pixel intersections. The complete-polygon denominator and valid-area numerator remain in square metres. The .80 threshold remains unrounded and fixed; no label-informed boundary exception or threshold change is allowed.
4. Export small tables containing administrative/source-piece IDs, valid area, weighted first and second moments and extraction/coverage diagnostics; merge moments locally into the 64 means + 64 population SD. Do not average per-tile SD or means without their area weights. Retain the sufficient statistics and transformation recipe so the final 128 descriptors and coverage decisions can be reconstructed without contacting EE again.
5. Archive the geometry/component identity, asset/version inventory, projection/overlap/mask recipe, export task/source-piece completeness ledger, downloaded tables and hashes, final descriptors and coverage/fallback ledger. These are the frozen experiment inputs. Do not require an archive of all intersecting 10 m / 64-band rasters in this first experiment. The 6,227 x 128 float64 descriptor values alone are 6,376,448 bytes; this is arithmetic for the final numeric matrix, **not** an estimate of all table/manifest bytes, remote pixels, runtime or billing.
6. In the authorized execution preflight, use a small geometry-selected pilot to verify the native grid, moment aggregation, overlap handling, precision/coverage behavior, actual destination/quota and compute footprint before scaling to all polygons. Asset access, source-piece enumeration, export destination, actual costs and wall time remain unverified. Resource limits and the concrete pilot/full-export release boundary must be recorded before bulk execution; no new billing/project/bucket configuration is implied by this proposal.

Trade-off: fitting and evaluation replay can use the frozen descriptor/statistics tables offline, but regenerating the tables from raw remote sensing data depends on continued availability of the pinned provider assets and EE processing. Saved derived hashes prove the frozen inputs used; they do not prove raw-pixel content identity when original rasters were not archived.

## Unselected alternative: local pixel extraction

Download or range-read the necessary 2017 raster windows/tiles, decode product values correctly, and compute polygon/pixel overlap and moments locally; retain the source pixels needed for independent extraction replay. This gives stronger control over intersection arithmetic and pixel-level reproducibility, while increasing transfer, storage and local geospatial processing. No raster footprint, compressed byte count, decoding implementation or runtime has been measured, so it must not be described as necessarily cheap or ready. Both choices preserve the same fixed year, 128 descriptors, .80 coverage rule, common cohorts and downstream model design.

The user chose the first experiment's statistical input tables with provider-dependent extraction replay, explicitly accepting the EE boundary-weight approximation and reproducibility limit. Local pixel-level extraction/archive is outside this first-experiment scope. Asset/destination/resource verification and concrete pilot/full-export boundaries remain to be frozen before execution.
